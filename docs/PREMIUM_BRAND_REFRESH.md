# SmartPrint premium cross-platform brand refresh

## Approved brand contract

- SmartPrint retains its own violet identity and semantic theme roles.
- Light primary accent: `#7258E8`; dark primary accent: `#9B8CFF`.
- The canonical SmartPrint mark is `apps/portal/public/smartprint-mark.svg`.
- Web and Windows use the same SVG from the shared portal public asset directory. Android adaptive foreground/monochrome artwork mirrors its printer geometry and uses the same violet gradient background.
- QuantaEdge is the parent-brand endorsement only, centered beneath the SmartPrint wordmark as smaller `by QuantaEdge` lettering. Quanta stays navy `#152038`; Edge stays orange `#F97316`. The endorsement sits directly on the transparent logo canvas: no white capsule, navy block, blue badge, or inserted QuantaEdge icon. On dark surfaces, a fine light outline keeps the navy Quanta lettering visible without changing its fill colour; on light surfaces, a fine dark outline keeps the orange Edge lettering readable.

## Customer experience

- Android Home has a clear first action, separate scan/import shortcuts, connected-shop status, recent files and an owned SmartPrint Photo Studio promotion.
- The home promotion is first-party only. No third-party advertising SDK, targeting, or document-content use is introduced.
- Activity groups print batches under full month names (for example, November 2026 and October 2026); order dates and file names are presented in human-readable form.
- Existing QR, local file, shop session, upload, validation, payment and print lifecycle actions remain the source of truth.

## Cross-platform acceptance

Review Web, Android and Windows in light and dark mode at phone, tablet and desktop widths. Check lockups, launcher/window icon, navigation, dialogs, cards, empty/loading/error states, text scaling, focus visibility and reduced motion. Verify that a theme switch changes surfaces and SmartPrint accents without recolouring the QuantaEdge parent signature or changing the SmartPrint logo asset.
