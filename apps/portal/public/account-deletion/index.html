ACCOUNT & PRIVACY

Delete your SmartPrint account

Sign in with the account you want to remove, review what deletion means, and confirm the request.

Verify your account

Use the same email and password you use in SmartPrint, or continue with the Google account linked to your SmartPrint customer profile. Your sign-in token is held in this page's memory only and is not saved in browser storage.

OR

Confirm account deletion

This action is permanent. Your customer login, account identity, provider link and refresh tokens will be removed. Your account will be unlinked from shop print jobs and sessions.

Shop-side print, payment, refund and reconciliation records may remain without your account link where needed to complete an order, resolve a dispute or meet legal recordkeeping duties. Documents already submitted to a shop follow the retention schedule in the Privacy Policy. Files saved only on this phone are not deleted by this request.

Account deletion completed

Your SmartPrint customer account and sign-in credentials have been deleted. The account reference has been removed from linked print sessions and print jobs. Shop-side transaction records may remain where the Privacy Policy explains they are needed.

Your locally stored My Files documents remain on this phone. Open SmartPrint → My Files to remove local documents, or uninstall the app to remove its app-private local storage.
