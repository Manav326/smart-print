PHOTO SHEETS · ID CARD PREPARATION

Prepare print-ready photo sheets with SmartPrint

SmartPrint Photo Studio helps customers and shop owners prepare photo layouts for A4 printing. The Android companion also includes ID Studio for scanning the front and back of a card and arranging both sides at a chosen physical size on an A4 sheet.

Explore all SmartPrint featuresRead about Private Printing

Photo Studio: size, frame and prepare an A4 sheet

Choose a supported photo size, adjust the crop and framing, set how many photos you need and preview how they fit on A4 pages. The source photo remains unchanged; SmartPrint creates a separate print-ready sheet for the job.

Four layout sizes

Prepare 35 × 45 mm, 25 × 35 mm, 2 × 2 inch and 4 × 6 inch portrait photo layouts. The shop can configure a price for each enabled size.

Crop and framing controls

Adjust the photo framing, zoom and focus position, then review the result in the A4 page preview.

Appearance controls

Use Natural, Warm, Crisp or Black & White looks, with brightness, contrast and saturation controls.

Background colour replacement

Sample a plain background colour and replace similar edge-connected pixels. This is a colour-key tool, not AI subject segmentation or automatic hair masking.

Names, DOB and custom text

Optionally print a name and date of birth below each photo, or add movable text layers to the A4 sheet.

Photo count and sheet density

Prepare 1–100 photos/copies, choose automatic fit or a selected number per A4 page, and inspect the page count before submission.

Photo Studio accepts JPG/JPEG/PNG originals up to 20 MB. It does not reshape or retouch facial features. Source-resolution guidance helps identify crops that may look soft at the chosen print size.

Photo orders from the customer or shop

Customers can open Photo Studio from the SmartPrint customer print flow. The shop can also create a photo order in its workspace for an existing linked customer or a walk-in recipient. Photo rates are configured by the shop, and the server calculates the quote.

Payment for shop-created photo orders starts as pending. The shop confirms the UPI receipt or cash received and explicitly dispatches the job; opening Photo Studio does not start printing.

Android ID Studio: prepare both sides of a card

ID Studio is part of the native SmartPrint Android app. It uses the supported Android document-scanner flow to capture the front and back, lets the customer review and rotate each side, and composes the two faces on one A4 portrait page at the selected physical dimensions with crop guides.

  • Review the front and reverse before saving the sheet.
  • Adjust supported card width and height in millimetres.
  • Preview both sides on the same A4 page with crop guides.
  • Keep prepared sheets in a separate Android Keystore-encrypted app-local vault, apart from My Files.
  • Open, view or delete saved prepared sheets on the device.
  • Prepared ID sheets are automatically classified as ID documents and always require Private Printing for a shop upload.

ID Studio prepares a printable layout. It does not verify identity, authenticate government documents or validate the information printed on a card.

Why ID Studio uses Private Printing

Prepared ID sheets are treated as privacy-sensitive documents. SmartPrint requires Private Printing for the entire batch containing the ID sheet. The shop must have a connected paired Windows Agent with its secure document key. The source is encrypted before transfer, is not placed in normal cloud document storage and is removed from the Agent after the shop confirms physical output and the Agent acknowledges deletion.

The shop still manages the job and payment status, but cannot preview the private source in its shop workspace. The customer's original and prepared ID sheet remain on the customer's own device until the customer chooses to delete them.

Photo and ID printing: common questions

Does SmartPrint make passport-size photos?

Photo Studio includes a 35 × 45 mm portrait ID-photo layout, as well as 25 × 35 mm, 2 × 2 inch and 4 × 6 inch layouts. The shop controls which sizes are priced and available.

Can I prepare a photo sheet with several copies?

Yes. Photo Studio supports 1–100 photos/copies and can calculate how they fit across A4 pages.

Does background replacement use AI?

No. The current tool samples a background colour and replaces similar pixels connected to the image edges. It is not AI subject segmentation.

Is ID Studio available on the web?

ID Studio is part of the native Android companion. The prepared ID sheet can be submitted through the shared SmartPrint customer workflow.

Can the shop preview a customer's ID Studio sheet?

No. Prepared ID sheets require Private Printing and their private source cannot be previewed by the shop. The shop still manages the order, payment and physical output.
