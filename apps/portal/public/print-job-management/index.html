FROM REQUEST TO OUTPUT

Print job management software that keeps the shop in control

SmartPrint connects customer document requests with the work a print shop needs to do next: inspect the file, confirm the quote, verify payment, approve the job, select a printer and confirm the output.

Keep each print request connected to its fulfilment

A print request is more than a file. The shop needs to know what the customer asked for, what was quoted, whether payment was confirmed, who approved the work and whether the output was completed. SmartPrint keeps those stages within one coordinated job workflow.

The shop remains the decision-maker. Staff review a job before dispatch, and the connected printer workflow only receives jobs that have been authorised.

A print-job lifecycle your team can follow

Receive

A customer starts from your QR station and submits a supported document with print details.

Review

Your staff inspect the document and request, then prepare a quote using the shop's pricing settings.

Confirm

The customer reviews the quote, and the shop follows its payment process and confirms receipt.

Approve and assign

The shop explicitly approves the job and assigns it to the selected printer.

Dispatch and confirm output

The connected Agent routes the job to the printer. The shop records whether output was completed.

Built for real shop-side control

SmartPrint connects a web-based shop workspace with the printer setup on the shop computer. The service coordinates customer sessions, job details, quotes, payment confirmation and authorisation. The Windows Agent handles the local printer connection and only executes work the shop has approved.

Document transfer can be local to the shop network or cloud-assisted, depending on the supported workflow. The cloud remains part of job coordination; SmartPrint is not an offline print-management system.

Print-job management: common questions

Can a shop review a job before it is printed?

Yes. Review and explicit shop approval are core parts of the SmartPrint print-job lifecycle.

Can the shop choose which printer receives a job?

The shop workspace supports printer assignment for authorised work through the connected Windows Agent.

Does SmartPrint automatically collect payment?

SmartPrint keeps payment status within the workflow, while the shop controls its payment process and confirms payment before dispatch.

Can staff track a job through completion?

The workflow connects the submitted request with review, approval, dispatch and output confirmation so staff can follow progress.
