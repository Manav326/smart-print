Privacy Policy

How SmartPrint handles account details, documents, print orders and campaign interactions.

1. Scope and who operates SmartPrint

This policy applies to SmartPrint by QuantaEdge, including the Windows desktop application and its background Windows Print Agent, the customer-facing Android application, SmartPrint website and customer portal, QR-based print journeys, and the services supporting customer accounts and print orders. The Windows Agent runs as a Windows service under the built-in NT AUTHORITY\\LocalService account so accepted print jobs and shop connectivity can continue while the desktop window is closed. “SmartPrint”, “we” and “us” refer to the service operated under the SmartPrint by QuantaEdge name.

SmartPrint connects customers with participating print shops. Customers may use the service as a guest or create an account using an email address and password or Google sign-in. The selected print shop is a separate business that receives the information needed to review and fulfil the print request.

2. Information that may be processed

Account and sign-in information

When you create or use a customer account, SmartPrint processes your display name, email address, password verifier (not your readable password), account identifier, sign-in provider details where you use Google, and authentication/session tokens. Google verifies the identity credential when you choose Google sign-in.

Documents and content you choose to submit

When you select files for a print request, the service may process the selected PDF or image content, original file name, file type and size, page and photo details, and print settings needed to prepare and fulfil the job. Your local My Files library, prepared scans and drafts are stored on your device until you choose to upload or remove them.

Print order and payment-related information

SmartPrint processes the selected shop and station, job and batch identifiers, print settings, quote and order amounts, job status, payment method and status, refund information, and transaction references that you or the shop provide. A print request may also include recipient details such as a name or phone number where that field is used. Customer print payments are handled through the shop's configured process, such as UPI or cash; SmartPrint does not ask you for a card number, card security code or bank password for those shop payments.

Windows desktop and Agent information

When you install, pair or use SmartPrint on Windows, the desktop app and background Agent process information needed to connect the shop computer and operate printing. This can include a machine/device identifier, Agent enrollment and authentication credentials, service health and connection status, printer names and capabilities, printer availability, print-job and batch identifiers, document names and technical properties, page counts, print settings, queue state and timestamps. The Agent also stores a local preference for the shop network selected by the owner. Where Windows makes it available, that preference can include the Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and adapter identifier; the Agent uses local IP address and subnet information to route phone-to-PC checks and reject requests arriving through a different network. The Agent sends its current local connection endpoint (IP address and port) to SmartPrint's cloud service over HTTPS so an enrolled phone can discover the paired shop computer. The Windows Agent may process the document content itself when it prepares and sends a print job to the Windows printing subsystem.

Service and campaign activity

To operate and protect the service, requests may be accompanied by technical information available to the website, API, hosting or security infrastructure, such as request time, route, IP address, browser or device information, and error/security events. The Windows Agent may send service health, enrollment, printer and job-status information to SmartPrint's cloud service over HTTPS. The Android app also reports campaign placement and interaction events such as an advertisement impression or click.

3. How information is used

  • Create and secure customer accounts, authenticate sign-ins and maintain sessions.
  • Connect you to the shop selected through its QR code; receive, validate and prepare documents you submit.
  • Calculate and display quotes, communicate job and payment status, and support print output, refunds, customer history and shop reconciliation.
  • Protect service availability, investigate faults or misuse, and maintain operational records.
  • Display SmartPrint campaign placements and measure campaign impressions and clicks.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting, tax, dispute-resolution and recordkeeping obligations.

4. When information is shared

Your selected print shop

When you submit a print request, the selected shop receives the job and document information needed to review, quote, approve and fulfil it. In Standard Printing, the shop workflow can make the submitted document available for review and printing. In Private Printing, the source document is encrypted to the paired shop Agent and is not made available for ordinary shop preview or download; the shop still receives job, quote, payment and output information needed to operate the order.

Service providers and sign-in services

SmartPrint uses service providers to host, secure and operate the website, API, database and document-transfer functions. Google services are used for Google sign-in and provide the Android document-scanner experience through Google Play services. Those providers process information as required to supply their services and under their applicable terms and privacy notices. The publisher must keep its actual provider list and processing locations accurate as the deployment changes.

Legal and business needs

Information may be disclosed when required by law or valid legal process, to protect users and the service, to investigate fraud or security incidents, or as part of a business reorganisation where permitted by law.

SmartPrint does not need your Google password, bank password or card security code to process an ordinary customer print order. If you choose an external payment app or website, that provider's own privacy terms apply to the information you give it.

5. Camera, scans and document handling

The QR scanner uses the camera to read a shop QR code on the device. QR decoding is performed on-device; camera frames are not uploaded just to decode the QR. The document-scanning interface is supplied through Google ML Kit Document Scanner in Google Play services. A scan remains in your local library until you choose to submit it for printing.

Windows Agent and shop-network transfer

The Windows Agent runs independently of the desktop window to maintain the print queue, discover printers and provide the local connection used by paired phones. It exposes its local API on TCP port 17842. The Agent binds remote requests to the shop network selected by the owner and rejects requests that arrive through a different local interface or subnet; sensitive upload, preview and print operations also require their existing pairing or one-time authorization. Standard direct Shop Wi-Fi document transfer uses SmartPrint's authenticated QSP2 encrypted document stream. The local HTTP endpoint is not the same as HTTPS, but the document stream is encrypted by the QSP2 protocol. Private Printing encrypts the source on the customer's device before transfer to the paired Agent. The Agent must process the document on the shop computer to print it, so a person with local Windows administrator or SYSTEM-level control, or access to the physical printer, may still be able to inspect or retain content.

Some Windows machine data, including the Agent identity, pairing/queue state and selected-network preference, is stored locally under the Windows ProgramData area. Credentials and protected queue material use application encryption and Windows protection/permissions. These machine-level files are intentionally kept outside the application package so updates and uninstall/reinstall do not automatically erase an accepted or unfinished job or the machine's pairing identity.

Standard document source files are subject to the SmartPrint service retention schedule. The current product configuration retains completed Standard Printing source documents for up to 72 hours after completion before scheduled purge. Private Printing source material is designed not to enter normal cloud document storage and is removed from the managed relay/Agent workflow after confirmed physical output. Unfinished, disputed or exceptional orders may require different handling to complete the service or meet legal obligations.

Standard direct Shop Wi-Fi upload uses the SmartPrint QSP2 authenticated encrypted document stream to the paired Agent. That local transfer endpoint may use HTTP on the shop's private network; this should not be confused with HTTPS transport to the public SmartPrint service. Use only a shop network you trust.

6. Sponsored placements and advertising

The Android app can display sponsored or campaign placements managed through SmartPrint. SmartPrint records campaign impression and click events to operate and measure those placements. A campaign may contain a destination link that you can choose to open. The advertising declaration in Google Play identifies that the app contains ads.

7. How long information is kept

  • Customer account and sign-in information is kept while the account is active and removed when the account is deleted, subject to the limited records described below.
  • Standard completed print-document sources are scheduled for purge within the current 72-hour post-completion retention window. Private Printing sources follow the removal process described above.
  • Shop-side job, quote, payment, refund, output and audit records may be retained after account deletion where needed to complete an order, handle a refund or dispute, reconcile shop payments, or satisfy applicable accounting, tax or legal obligations. Account references are removed from print-session and print-job records when the customer account is deleted; this does not erase the shop's underlying transaction record.
  • Security, operational and campaign event records are kept for the period reasonably needed for service operation, security and reporting, subject to applicable retention settings and legal obligations.
  • Files stored only in the Android app's local library are not removed by remote account deletion. You can remove them in My Files or uninstall the app.
  • Windows machine-level Agent data, such as its device identity, selected-network preference and protected queue state, is stored separately from the app package and may remain after the Windows app is uninstalled. Uninstalling removes the packaged app/service but does not automatically erase all machine-level SmartPrint data. Contact the publisher for approved cleanup instructions before decommissioning or transferring a shop PC.
Important: Deleting a SmartPrint account is not the same as cancelling or deleting a print job already submitted to a shop. Contact the selected shop about an active order, cancellation or refund.

8. Security

SmartPrint uses password hashing rather than storing readable passwords, access-controlled APIs, HTTPS for public service connections, Android Keystore-backed encryption for locally stored account credentials and customer-history snapshots, Windows DPAPI/application encryption and restrictive file permissions for protected Agent data, and encrypted QSP2 document transfer for the supported direct-LAN Agent upload. Private Printing uses end-to-end encryption between the customer device and paired Agent. No online service or device can be guaranteed completely secure; keep your device protected and do not share sign-in codes or tokens.

9. Your choices and account deletion

You can use the Account screen in the Android app to open this policy, sign out or permanently delete your customer account. You can also use the public SmartPrint account deletion page from a browser. The web page supports email/password and Google sign-in so that you can request deletion even when you cannot access the app.

When deletion succeeds, SmartPrint removes the customer account identity and authentication material and unlinks the account from print-session and print-job records. Shop-side operational, payment, refund and legally required transaction records may remain without the account link for the reasons explained in section 7. Files stored locally on your phone remain there until you remove them. Documents already submitted to a shop follow the document and transaction retention rules described above.

You may contact SmartPrint to ask about access, correction or other privacy matters, subject to identity verification and applicable law. You may also have rights under the data-protection laws that apply where you live.

10. Children

SmartPrint is a general customer and business service, not a service designed to collect information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should contact the publisher using the contact channel below so the request can be reviewed.

We may update this policy when the app, services, legal requirements or data practices change. The effective date and version at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate service channel where required.
