Make document submission easier at a busy counter

At a photocopy or Xerox counter, a customer may arrive with a document on a phone and need a quick printout. The shop still needs to check the file, understand the requested print settings, calculate the price and decide when the job is ready. A QR-based submission flow gives the customer one clear starting point while keeping those decisions with the shop.

With SmartPrint, the customer scans the participating shop's QR code and submits a supported document. The request appears in the shop workspace for review. The QR scan does not send an unapproved job straight to a printer.

One QR entry point

Display a shop or station QR code at the counter. Customers scan it to open the correct shop-specific document-submission flow.

Requests in a shop workspace

Staff can review incoming documents and the requested print details together before preparing a quote.

Shop-controlled pricing and approval

Your team sets the price, confirms payment through its process and authorises the work before dispatch.

Use the printers already installed

The SmartPrint Windows Agent connects the shop computer to its installed Windows printer queues and routes authorised jobs.

How a QR print order moves through the shop

1. Display the station QR code

Place the shop's QR code where customers can see it. Add a short instruction such as: “Scan to submit your document. Wait for the shop to confirm your quote.”

2. Customer submits the document

The customer scans the code, opens the shop-specific page and selects a supported file from their phone.

3. Staff review the request

Check the document and requested print settings. Clarify anything that is missing before quoting or starting the job.

4. Prepare the quote and confirm payment

Apply the shop's own pricing. Confirm payment according to the shop's process and explicitly approve the job.

5. Dispatch and confirm the output

Send the authorised job to the selected connected printer. Confirm the actual print handover in the shop workflow.

A simple way to introduce QR ordering at your counter

Start with one counter or one busy station rather than changing every customer interaction at once. Test the full flow with a shop-owned sample document. Ask staff to practise reviewing the file, entering the quote, confirming payment, authorising dispatch and confirming output.

  • Make the sign readable: place the QR code at eye level and leave enough space for a phone camera to scan it.
  • Explain the next step: tell customers to wait for the shop's quote and approval instead of assuming a scan means printing has started.
  • Keep staff in control: nominate who reviews incoming jobs and who confirms payment and dispatch.
  • Check the printer connection: pair the Windows Agent with the shop workspace and test the installed printer queues.
  • Review the handover: make sure staff can tell whether output was physically completed or still needs attention.

What SmartPrint supports today

SmartPrint is designed for participating local print shops and their customers. Current public V1 limits are intentionally clear:

  • Supported files: PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG.
  • File limit: up to 20 MB per job.
  • PDF limit: up to 500 pages.
  • Payment: the shop confirms customer payment; SmartPrint does not automatically charge the customer's payment account.
  • Printing: the Windows Agent connects installed Windows printer queues. It does not replace the shop's printer or driver setup.
  • Connectivity: local-network document transfer is supported, but the complete session, quote and job workflow still requires the SmartPrint service.

SmartPrint is print-shop workflow software. It is not a full cyber-cafe billing system, a public directory of print providers or an independent print-fulfilment service.

Questions from Xerox and copy-shop owners

Can customers submit a document from their phone?

Yes. Customers scan the participating shop's QR code and submit a supported PDF or image file through the shop-specific SmartPrint flow.

Does scanning the QR code start printing automatically?

No. Staff review the request, prepare the quote, confirm payment and explicitly approve the job before it is sent to the connected printer workflow.

Do I need to replace my current printer?

No. SmartPrint's Windows Agent connects to the printer queues installed on the shop's Windows computer. The printer and its drivers remain the shop's equipment.

Can I set up more than one customer counter?

SmartPrint supports shop QR stations. A shop can use station-specific QR entry points for its customer handoff.

Does SmartPrint work fully offline?

No. Local-network document transfer is supported, but shop sessions, quotes and job coordination still rely on the SmartPrint service.
