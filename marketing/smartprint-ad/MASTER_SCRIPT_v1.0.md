PRODUCTION VERSION 1.0  •  11 NOVEMBER 2026


1. Creative direction
The audience should first feel the inconvenience, then recognise the human cost of mistakes, then discover SmartPrint as a practical solution. The advertisement is a short film with a product reveal — not a software tutorial.
	WHAT THE VIEWER FEELS
	WHAT THE VIEWER UNDERSTANDS

	“This print-shop process is frustrating.”

“Small mistakes waste time and paper.”

“Someone has finally simplified it.”
	Customers can submit documents through a shop Upload QR, choose print preferences per file, and send an organised request. Shop owners review the request and deliberately authorise printing.


Creative rules
Use the white Mahindra XUV500 in every car shot. It must remain the same vehicle, colour and general appearance across clips.
Use the uploaded photograph as the protagonist identity reference. Preserve the real face, age, skin tone, hairline and natural proportions; do not beautify, age-shift or substitute the face.
The shop owner and the second customer must each have one fixed character reference portrait reused across all their clips.
Generate video footage with neutral/blank phone and monitor screens. Do not ask a video model to draw SmartPrint screens, QR codes, WhatsApp text, app-store listings, logos or readable copy.
All Hindi dialogue and voice-over should be recorded separately in post. Generate clean silent footage or ambience-only footage; this avoids inaccurate Hindi, lip-sync drift and inconsistent voices.
Use actual SmartPrint QA captures only as very brief edited inserts in the relevant phone/desktop screen area. The product UI must support the story, never dominate the film.
No promises of zero mistakes, instant printing, automatic printing on upload, universal printer compatibility, or standard LAN end-to-end encryption.
2. Brand lockup — mandatory correction
Do not use the current distorted combined logo artwork. Do not use the Q-shaped QuantaEdge icon or any standalone Q symbol in this advertisement.
For all brand moments, use a clean, single-line typographic lockup: “SmartPrint by QuantaEdge”. Keep “by QuantaEdge” on the same horizontal line as SmartPrint. Use the approved QuantaEdge wordmark lettering only if supplied; no Q icon, no parent-brand badge, no white box, no bevel, no stretched SVG, and no AI-generated logo.
Recommended treatment: SmartPrint in the SmartPrint violet identity; “by QuantaEdge” immediately to its right in a smaller but readable weight. Keep the entire lockup on one baseline. If the exact approved QuantaEdge wordmark file is unavailable, typeset the words cleanly and consistently rather than modifying a distorted logo.
3. Continuity bible for every AI clip
	Protagonist
	Use the user-uploaded face photo as the identity reference. Same face and build throughout. Wardrobe lock: premium, understated dark-navy casual shirt, charcoal trousers, simple watch; avoid changing outfit between print shop, car and home unless the scene requires a jacket (not recommended).

	Car
	White Mahindra XUV500 SUV; same vehicle across all outdoor shots; clean but not showroom-perfect; no readable registration plate, no added decals or unusual accessories.

	Shop owner
	Indian male, approximately late 40s/early 50s, approachable, experienced local print-shop owner; light-blue checked shirt; same face, hair, build and shirt across scenes.

	Second customer
	Indian male, approximately 30s, beige/olive shirt; frustrated but not aggressive; same face, outfit and papers in the argument scene.

	Print shop
	Believable, tidy Indian neighbourhood print/copy shop; printer, paper stacks, counter and desktop PC; warm neutral light; not luxurious, not dirty, no brand signage.

	Home office
	Quiet, modern Indian home desk; laptop, notebook, pen and warm evening light; no extra people; no visible third-party brand marks.

	Visual grade
	Premium Indian commercial film, photorealistic, restrained warm highlights, natural skin texture, controlled contrast, shallow depth of field for close-ups, realistic motion blur.

	Frame
	16:9 landscape master, 4K preferred, 24 fps, natural shutter/motion. Keep key faces and props within a central 80% safe area for later 9:16 and 1:1 adaptations.

	Performance
	Underplayed, conversational and believable. Avoid exaggerated comedy, melodramatic anger, theatrical hand gestures or overacting.


4. Master negative prompt
Append this to every video prompt: “No generated text, no subtitles, no captions, no logos, no Q icon, no fake brand marks, no fabricated readable phone or desktop interface, no legible QR code, no WhatsApp logo or copied interface, no incorrect car colour, no vehicle model changes, no face morphing, no extra fingers, no duplicated hands, no floating documents, no sudden wardrobe changes, no scene cuts, no montage, no watermarks, no exaggerated acting.”
Each clip is one continuous shot with one primary action. Do not ask the model to create multiple camera cuts within a 10-second generation.
5. Full 20-clip shooting script
Target master length: approximately 2 minutes 45 seconds to 3 minutes after handles and pauses are trimmed. Generate 20 independent clips, each at 10 seconds. Keep the sequence number in every filename: SP_AD_01.mp4, SP_AD_02.mp4, and so on.
